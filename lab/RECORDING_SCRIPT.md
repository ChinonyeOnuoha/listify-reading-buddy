# Reading Buddy AI lab — recording script

Six short recordings, one per case. Each takes about 30–60 seconds. Record on the device you want to test (a phone's voice memos app is fine), then upload each file in the lab and pick the matching case.

**Passage (original, 118 words):**

> Every evening, Mrs Okafor carried a small brass lantern to the end of Willow Lane. She set it on the low stone wall beside the post box, so that anyone walking home after dark could find the gate to the community garden. Children liked to guess how long the flame would last. Some said an hour; others swore it burned until morning. Nobody ever checked. What mattered was the warm circle of light it made on the path, and the way strangers slowed down to say good night as they passed. One windy night the lantern went out early, and the next day three neighbours arrived with new wicks, a bottle of oil and a box of matches.

| # | Case | While recording | What a good result notices |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal reading | Read as written at your usual pace. | Few or no differences. Differences here are most likely recognition errors. |
| 2 | Repetitions and restarts | Restart “carried a small brass… **a small brass** lantern”; say “Some said **said** an hour”; restart “Nobody ever… **Nobody ever** checked.” | All three repeats kept in the transcript. |
| 3 | Omit / add / substitute | Leave out **low** (“on the stone wall”); add **very** (“the very warm circle”); say **village** for “community”; say **two** for “three” neighbours. | One omission, one addition, two substitutions. |
| 4 | Pause in a sentence | Pause ~2 seconds inside “the warm circle of … light”. | A ~2 s pause between “of” and “light” (other natural pauses will show too). |
| 5 | Pace change | First three sentences slowly; from “Some said an hour” to the end, noticeably faster. | Higher words-per-minute after “Some said an hour” — only if timestamps look reliable. |
| 6 | Silence / noise | ~5 s silence, ~10 s background noise without speaking, then read only the first sentence with the noise still going. | No invented words during the silent/noise-only part. |

**Tips:** keep the phone 20–30 cm away; one take per case is enough. Recordings stay on your device — the lab processes them in the browser and doesn't upload them.
